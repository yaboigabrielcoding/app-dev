# 🎬 My Favorite Series
## 📺 Series Title
**Breaking Bad**

## 📖 Description
*Breaking Bad* follows Walter White, a high school chemistry teacher who, after being diagnosed with cancer, turns to manufacturing drugs to secure his family’s future. As he partners with former student Jesse Pinkman, he becomes deeply involved in the dangerous world of crime.

## ⭐ Why I Like It
### 🧠 Great Storytelling
The plot is carefully written, with each episode building on the previous one. Small details often become important later, making the story feel connected and meaningful.

### 👤 Strong Characters
The characters feel real and complex. Walter White’s transformation from a normal teacher into a powerful and dangerous figure is one of the most interesting character developments.

### ⚖️ Moral Lessons
The series shows how choices can affect a person’s life. It makes you question what is right and wrong, and how far someone is willing to go for their goals.

### 🔥 Intensity and Suspense
The show keeps you engaged with unexpected twists and high-stakes situations, making it exciting to watch from start to finish.
